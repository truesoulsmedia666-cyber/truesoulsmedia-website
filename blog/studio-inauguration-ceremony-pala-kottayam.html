A New Chapter Begins: Inside Our Studio Inauguration & Blessing Ceremony in Pala

Noel George & Abin J Antony

Co-Founders, True Souls Media LLP · November 2026 · 5 min read

🪔 Lighting the Nilavilakku — True Souls Media Flagship Studio, Pala, Kottayam Visit Studio in Maps ↗

Every frame we have ever shot at True Souls Media has been about one thing: emotion. We have stood behind the lens at over 200 weddings across Kerala—watching parents hold back tears of pride, elders offer silent prayers, and families gather around the sacred flame of a Nilavilakku to bless a new beginning.

This time, the camera was turned toward our own family. Stepping inside our newly completed creative studio at Njondimakkal Junction on the Pala-Thodupuzha Road in Kottayam, we gathered with our parents, grandparents, elders, and core team to light the traditional lamp and dedicate our new creative home.

Here is a behind-the-scenes look at our inauguration day—the emotions, the people who made it possible, and what this new space means for every couple, creator, and brand we serve.

01. Beginning with Light, Prayer & Gratitude

In Kerala’s culture, no meaningful venture begins without lighting the Nilavilakku—a symbol of dispelling darkness and welcoming wisdom, warmth, and prosperity. Seeing the golden flames flicker in front of the “True Souls Media” lettering on our studio wall was a surreal moment for all of us.

When we started True Souls Media in 2022, our office was wherever our camera bags and laptops were—between wedding venues in Kottayam, late-night editing tables, and coffee shop strategy meetings. Watching that journey culminate in a permanent, purpose-built creative headquarters in the heart of Pala reminded us that great dreams are built one honest story at a time.

A Moment of Grace: Our founders, team members, parents, and elders gathered in prayer around the lit brass lamp inside our new Client Consultation & Reception Lounge, with our glass-partitioned Editing & Production Suite visible alongside.

02. Surrounded by the True Souls Who Built Us

Our motto—visible right on the studio’s interior wall—is “Capturing True Souls.” We chose that name because we believe media isn’t about flashy gimmicks; it’s about honoring real people and real relationships.

Having our parents, elders, and extended family stand shoulder-to-shoulder with our young creative crew in the consultation room was the highlight of the inauguration. They are the ones who believed in us during the late-night edits, the back-to-back wedding seasons, and the leap into full-scale event management and digital marketing.

Generations Together: Smiles, blessings, and proud moments as our family and creative team celebrate the official opening of True Souls Media LLP in Pala.
“When a family walks into our studio to plan their daughter’s or son’s wedding, we want them to feel the exact same warmth, respect, and togetherness that filled this room on our inauguration day.”
— Noel George & Abin J Antony, Co-Founders

03. Inside the Space: Designed for Collaboration & Craft

As you can see in the photos from our ceremony, every square foot of the Pala studio has been thoughtfully zoned to bridge hospitality with high-end production:

  • The Executive Consultation Desk & Family Lounge: Outfitted with plush seating, warm ambient curtains, and a dedicated presentation desk where couples, parents, and business owners can sit down together to review wedding mood boards, sample heirloom albums, and marketing roadmaps.
  • The Glass-Partitioned Editing & Color Suite: Right behind the main consultation desk sits our sound-insulated production bay, allowing clients to watch their wedding trailers, reels, and commercial cuts come to life on color-calibrated displays.
  • The Broadcast Podcast Studio: A dedicated, acoustically treated multi-camera 4K recording room built for entrepreneurs, doctors, educators, and creators across Kottayam and Central Travancore.
