True Souls Media Opens Brand-New Studio in Pala, Kottayam

Noel George & Abin J Antony

Co-Founders, True Souls Media LLP · October 2026 · 4 min read

📍 True Souls Media Flagship Studio — Pala-Thodupuzha Rd, Kottayam, Kerala Open in Maps ↗

We are thrilled to announce a monumental milestone in the journey of True Souls Media LLP: the official opening of our brand-new creative studio in Pala, Kottayam, Kerala!

From our beginnings in 2022 capturing weddings and crafting digital brands across Kerala, our dream has always been to build a physical creative sanctuary. A space where couples can sit down in comfort to design their once-in-a-lifetime celebrations, where creators can record studio-grade podcasts, and where our post-production artists can craft cinematic magic in real-time.

Our new studio, situated right on the Pala-Thodupuzha Road at Njondimakkal Junction, brings this vision to life with three specialized zones: a dedicated Podcast Area, an inviting Client Reception Lounge, and an elite High-Performance Editing Hub.

1. The Dedicated Podcast Area

Podcast and video content consumption in Kerala is booming. Whether you are an entrepreneur building personal authority, a company launching an industry talk show, or a wedding couple recording an intimate memory series with your parents, audio quality can make or break your reach.

Our new studio hosts an acoustically treated, broadcast-ready Podcast Studio equipped with:

  • Broadcast Audio Microphones & DSP: High-end cardioid microphones paired with dedicated preamps to capture crystal-clear, warm voice tone with zero echo.
  • Multi-Angle 4K Cinema Cameras: Ultra-HD camera setups positioned for dynamic switching between host, guest, and wide room perspectives.
  • Custom Mood Lighting & Soundproofing: Acoustic foam treatment, ambient neon/warm lighting, and a distraction-free recording environment.
  • Turnkey Post-Production & Distribution: We handle end-to-end editing, audio mastering, sound effects, short-form reels for Instagram/YouTube Shorts, and distribution onto Spotify & Apple Podcasts.

2. Welcoming Client Reception & Consultation Lounge

Planning a wedding or executing a multi-channel digital marketing campaign should never feel stressful. We designed our front Reception and Consultation Lounge as a comfortable, creative retreat where you can relax, have a coffee, and explore ideas with us in person.

  • Large-Screen 4K Portfolio Previews: View full-length wedding films, album layouts, pre-wedding concepts, and stage decor renders in high fidelity.
  • Face-to-Face Creative Alignment: Sit down directly with our co-founders Noel George (Creative Director) and Abin J Antony (Strategy Lead) to craft transparent timelines and budgets.
  • Touch-and-Feel Sample Albums: Browse our premium leather and acrylic photo albums, custom calendars, and cinematic print finishes before finalizing your package.

3. High-Performance Editing & Color Grading Hub

Speed and precision define modern visual storytelling. In the past, video editing took weeks of back-and-forth communication. Our new in-house Editing Hub houses workstation-grade editing rigs, hardware-calibrated color grading displays, and high-speed NVMe storage arrays.

What this means for our clients:

  • Lightning-Fast Wedding Highlights: Same-day teaser cuts and next-day Instagram reels edited live by our on-site editors.
  • Color-Accurate Master Grading: Cinematic LUTs and custom grading suited for both traditional Kerala temple lighting and modern chandelier convention halls.
  • Live Client Reviews: Drop into our Pala editing suite to review initial video drafts, select music tracks, or tweak cuts with the editor side-by-side.

Why Pala, Kottayam?

Pala is strategically located at the intersection of Kerala’s cultural heritage and modern entrepreneurship. With seamless connectivity to Kottayam town, Ernakulam, Idukki, and Kumarakom, setting up our flagship base here allows our production crew to deploy rapidly to venues anywhere across all 14 districts of Kerala.

Most importantly, it gives our clients in Central Travancore an accessible, inspiring place to walk in, brainstorm, and watch their vision turn into reality.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where exactly is the studio in Pala?

We are located at Njondimakkal Junction, on the main Pala-Thodupuzha Road, Pala, Kerala 686575. You can easily navigate via Google Maps by searching for True Souls Media LLP or clicking the map button on this page.

Can creators book the podcast area for a single episode?

Yes. We offer hourly studio rentals as well as monthly episode packages. Our production crew handles audio recording, camera switching, audio mastering, and thumbnail exports.

Do we need an appointment to visit for wedding planning?

Walk-ins are always welcome during business hours, but we recommend booking a free 30-minute consultation slot in advance so our creative directors Noel George or Abin J Antony can dedicate their undivided time to your event vision.

Can I sit with the video editor to review our wedding film cuts?

Yes! Our Editing Hub is designed to accommodate live client review sessions where you can sit side-by-side with our colorist and video editor to fine-tune your song selections and timeline highlights.
